# Contributing evidence

We welcome corrections, documented counterexamples, and experimental criticism.

## Atlas submission checklist
- Identify the **specific feature**, not merely a brand or industry.
- Provide a primary source or a directly verifiable technical record.
- Classify the technique conservatively: machine learning, generative AI, or unclear.
- Include a plain-language limitation. A company's technical claim is not an independent audit.
- Never imply every feature in a product uses AI.
- Provide a stable URL and the date the claim was reviewed.

## Evidence Lab submission checklist
- Separate proposal, registered, in progress, completed, and independently replicated.
- Publish task definitions and acceptance criteria **before** collecting outcomes.
- Record model/version, prompt, tools, human decisions, run environment, duration and failures.
- Obtain informed consent for studies of people; do not publish private inputs or participant identifiers.
- Include negative controls and results that disagree with the hypothesis.
- Do not describe simulations or illustrative examples as observed empirical findings.

Edit `site/data/*.json` and update research documents as needed. CI checks basic internal consistency. It does not check whether a linked source genuinely supports the claim; reviewers must do that work.

Keep discussion civil. Debate evidence rather than individuals.
